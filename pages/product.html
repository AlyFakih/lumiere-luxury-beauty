Product Details

Our Radiance Serum is a powerful brightening treatment designed to transform dull, uneven skin. This advanced formula combines potent actives with skin-loving ingredients to deliver visible results without irritation.

The lightweight, silky texture absorbs quickly, making it perfect for layering in your skincare routine. With regular use, skin appears more luminous, even-toned, and youthful.

Features:

  • Proprietary brightening complex targets hyperpigmentation
  • 15% Vitamin C complex for antioxidant protection
  • Multi-weight hyaluronic acid provides deep hydration
  • Niacinamide strengthens skin barrier
  • Free from parabens, sulfates, and phthalates
  • Dermatologist tested and approved
  • Not tested on animals

Full Ingredient List

Vitamin C (Ascorbic Acid)

A powerful antioxidant that brightens skin, protects against environmental damage, and boosts collagen production.

Niacinamide

Improves skin texture, minimizes pore appearance, and strengthens the skin barrier while reducing discoloration.

Hyaluronic Acid

Attracts and retains moisture, plumping the skin and reducing the appearance of fine lines.

Licorice Root Extract

Natural brightening agent that helps reduce the appearance of dark spots and discoloration.

Application Instructions

1

Cleanse

Start with clean, dry skin. Apply your favorite gentle cleanser and pat dry.

2

Apply

Dispense 3-4 drops onto fingertips and gently press into face and neck. Avoid direct contact with eyes.

3

Follow with Moisturizer

Allow serum to fully absorb (30-60 seconds), then follow with your favorite moisturizer.

4

Sun Protection

Always apply sunscreen in the morning after using this product, as vitamin C can increase sun sensitivity.

Recommended Use: Apply morning and evening for best results. If used in the morning, always follow with SPF protection.

Tip: Store in a cool, dark place to maintain potency of active ingredients.
