Our Story

Crafting luxury beauty with passion and purpose since 2005

The Lumière Legacy

Founded in Paris in 2005 by renowned dermatologist Sophie Laurent, Lumière was born from a belief that luxury beauty should combine scientific innovation with natural ingredients to deliver transformative results.

After years of working with patients struggling to find effective yet gentle skincare solutions, Sophie embarked on a journey to create formulations that would meet the highest standards of efficacy and luxury.

What began as a small collection of bespoke skincare formulations created in her Parisian laboratory has evolved into a globally recognized beauty brand that stands for excellence, integrity, and timeless elegance.

Our Philosophy

At Lumière, we believe that true beauty lies in the harmonious balance between science and nature, tradition and innovation. Every product we create embodies this philosophy, delivering an experience that transcends ordinary beauty routines.

Scientific Innovation

Our team of scientists continuously pushes the boundaries of beauty science to create formulations that deliver visible results.

Natural Luxury

We carefully source the finest natural ingredients from around the world, ensuring each component meets our standard of excellence.

Ethical Approach

Our commitment to sustainability and ethical practices extends to every aspect of our business, from sourcing to packaging.

Artistry & Craftsmanship

Ingredient Selection

Our ingredients are handpicked from the most pristine locations around the world. From French lavender fields to Japanese tea gardens, we source only the finest raw materials.

Formulation Process

Each Lumière formula requires months of meticulous development and testing. Our laboratories combine traditional techniques with cutting-edge technology to create products of unparalleled quality.

Packaging Design

Our packaging is a testament to our commitment to sustainability and luxury. Each container is designed to be both aesthetically pleasing and environmentally responsible.

Quality Assurance

Every Lumière product undergoes rigorous quality testing to ensure it meets our exacting standards. We never compromise on quality, ensuring each item delivers the Lumière experience.
