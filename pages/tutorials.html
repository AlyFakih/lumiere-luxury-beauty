Expert Quick Tips

Store Eye Products in the Refrigerator

Keep your eye creams and serums in the refrigerator to enhance their de-puffing effects and extend their shelf life.

Sophie LaurentFounder

Apply Skincare to Damp Skin

For maximum absorption, apply your hydrating serums and moisturizers to slightly damp skin, within 60 seconds after cleansing.

Antoine DuboisChief Scientist

The "Less is More" Foundation Rule

Start with a small amount of foundation and build coverage only where needed. This creates a more natural, skin-like finish.

Isabelle ChenCreative Director
